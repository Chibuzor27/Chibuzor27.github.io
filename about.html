About Quell Publishers

We are a publishing firm founded on the belief that literature plays a unique role in shaping minds. A space where diverse and unique voices ignite imagination through the power of words has thus been created at Quell Publishers

1
Our Mission

Quell Publishers' mission is to nurture a love for reading and learning by providing a diverse range of publications that inspire, educate, and entertain. We endeavor to support authors by giving them a platform to share their voices and ideas with a global audience

2
Our Vision

Quell Publishers envisions a world where knowledge and creativity are accessible to everyone. Our goal is to be at the apex of leveraging technology and innovative practices to expand our reach and impact

3
Our Values

  • Quell Publishers is committed to maintaining the highest standards in every publication
  • Quell Publishers upholds ethical practices in all our dealings, ensuring transparency and fairness

4
What We Do

Quell Publishers is immersed in a wide variety of genres, from fiction and non-fiction to poetry. Our catalog features work from both established authors and emerging talents

5
How We Do It

Quell Publishers embraces the digital age, offering ebooks, audiobooks, and online publications to meet the needs of today's readers and readers of yore
